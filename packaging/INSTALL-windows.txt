Progrezzor – installation on Windows (internal test build)
==========================================================

The archive contains Progrezzor in two plug-in formats, CLAP and VST®3.
Install the one your DAW uses, or both. Progrezzor has so far only been
built and tested on Linux, so on Windows it is not tried yet.

For your user only (no administrator rights needed):

    CLAP:  copy Progrezzor.clap to   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\CLAP\
    VST3:  copy the Progrezzor.vst3 folder to   %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3\

For all users (needs administrator rights):

    CLAP:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
    VST3:  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\

Create the folder if it does not exist, then restart your DAW or rescan
plug-ins.

If the Progrezzor window looks blurry, is the wrong size or is cut off when
Windows display scaling is above 100 %, your DAW is probably stretching the
plug-in window. Turn that DAW setting off for Progrezzor. In Ableton Live:
right-click Progrezzor in the browser and untick "Auto-Scale Plug-in
Window", then reopen the plug-in.

How to use it:

  - Put Progrezzor on a MIDI track and a synth after it. The notes come out
    of Progrezzor and go on to the synth. Progrezzor also has a small sound
    of its own, so you can hear what you play. Turn it off with the sound
    chooser (Off) when your synth plays the notes.
  - Click the pads, or play the white keys of a MIDI keyboard.
  - Drag a MIDI file out of the Drag tile into your DAW. The same file is
    saved in the Progrezzor folder in your music folder (Open Folder).

Your own settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Progrezzor\. Removing the plug-in
leaves them in place.
