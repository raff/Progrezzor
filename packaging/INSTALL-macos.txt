Progrezzor – installation on macOS
==================================

The archive contains Progrezzor in two plug-in formats, CLAP and VST3, for
both Apple silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or newer).
Install the one your DAW uses, or both.

CLAP:

    mkdir -p ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP
    cp -r Progrezzor.clap ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/

VST3:

    mkdir -p ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
    cp -r Progrezzor.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

(Use /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/ instead to install for all users.)

The Mac build is not signed. macOS may say it cannot check the plug-in. Remove
the download mark once, then restart your DAW or rescan plug-ins:

    xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/Progrezzor.clap
    xattr -cr ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Progrezzor.vst3

How to use it:

  - Put Progrezzor on a MIDI track and a synth after it. The notes come out
    of Progrezzor and go on to the synth. Progrezzor also has a small sound
    of its own, so you can hear what you play. Turn it off with the Sound menu
    (Off) when your synth plays the notes.
  - Click the pads, or play the white keys of a MIDI keyboard.
  - Drag a MIDI file out of the Drag tile into your DAW. The same file is
    saved in the Progrezzor folder in your music folder (Open Folder).

Your own settings are saved in ~/Library/Application Support/Progrezzor/.
Removing the plug-in leaves them in place.
